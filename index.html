Unit 4 Curriculum Resource • State Constitutional Structure & Local Governance

I. The Washington State Constitution

Ratified by voters and admitted to the Union as the 42nd state, Washington's governing document establishes a separation of powers across the Executive, Legislative, and Judicial branches.

Key Exam Concept: Article I, Section 7 of the Washington State Constitution provides broader privacy protections concerning "private affairs" than the federal Fourth Amendment.

II. Legislative Structure & Municipal Codes

The bicameral legislature consists of 49 legislative districts, each electing one senator and two representatives. Local municipalities in Snohomish and King counties operate under First-Class, Second-Class, or Optional Municipal Code classifications.
